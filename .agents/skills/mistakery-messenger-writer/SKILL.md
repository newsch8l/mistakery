# Mistakery Messenger Writer

Write behaviour, not a professional persona. A sender is recognizable through what they did, want, risk, and demand from the founder; casing, emoji, slang, and terminal garnish only reinforce that.

## Guardrail

Use this skill only after the situation, mechanics, sender, and two consequences are approved. Do not invent plot, change `cards.json`, edit the UI, or treat audition copy as approved production copy.

If the situation cannot be explained without abstract technical language or an authorial formulation of its theme, stop the copy pass and return the situation for simplification. Do not make prose compensate for an action ordinary players cannot picture.

## Read the right context

Read `MISTAKERY_START_HERE.md` and `docs/core/REJECTED_PATTERNS.md` as scope guards. For copy decisions, use this source priority:

1. **State Bible** — facts of the world, stage, eligibility, sender knowledge, and mechanics.
2. **Character Bible and TOV Bible** — current character, readable voice, and anti-voice rules.
3. **Voice engine:** [character-voice-engines.md](references/character-voice-engines.md) — the current sender's behavioral range, pressure modes, emotional states, nearest neighbor, and anti-voice.
4. **Current deck:** read every current card from the sender in `cards.json`, plus adjacent story cards, to avoid repeats.

Then read `MISTAKERY_MESSENGER_WRITER_FULL_CHARACTER_AUDITION.md` as the author-approved liveliness calibration. Use it to judge emotional force, messenger messiness, and character distance. Do not copy its wording, phrases, syntax, line structure, profanity placement, or joke construction. It is a quality floor, not a template.

For Package A, read `MISTAKERY_STAGE_2B_PACKAGE_A_COPY_AUDITION.md` as an author-approved clarity and liveliness level only. Its exact cards await production integration; never copy their phrases, syntax, line structure, or joke construction into new work.

State Bible wins fact conflicts; Character Bible and TOV Bible win current-character conflicts. The voice engine expands those rules without overriding them. For Package A, also read the two Stage 2B documents; for prior voice decisions, read the listed Checkpoint 5 documents. Then read [project-reading-map.md](references/project-reading-map.md), [taste-memory.md](references/taste-memory.md), and [public-messenger-corpus.md](references/public-messenger-corpus.md) only for rhythm observation.

The PDF with examples, `docs/source/MISTAKERY_VALIDATION_CHARACTERS_WITH_EXAMPLES.pdf`, belongs to **Editor**, after creative selection. PDF examples are not production copy. Editor uses them only to check whether the survivors sit inside the author's broad range of liveliness, relationship, pressure, and messenger rhythm. The PDF is not Writer's construction source and never donates wording, syntax, events, brands, companies, or mechanics.

## Writer

Writer creates five vivid candidates before any editorial scoring. Give Writer a short creative brief containing the **full character name and handle**, approved situation, fact lock, what the sender wants, and the available emotional and pressure range.

Write the first pass as the character under pressure, not as a copywriter demonstrating the Character Bible. Preserve anger, panic, vanity, pettiness, tenderness, manipulation, slang, mistakes, and uneven rhythm when they belong to this sender. Do not make the line safer, wiser, cleaner, or more balanced than the person sending it.

Visible messenger copy does not use semicolons. They read as composed prose rather than spontaneous chat. Profanity is rare across the deck and appears only at a real emotional spike. It is never a quota, a punchline by itself, or a shortcut to character.

Investor @unicorn_hunter pressures through money, ownership, trend fear, and status. He can be loud and humiliating, but he is not routinely rude or coarse and does not rely on profanity. His threat should sound like a rich man treating a small cheque as authority, not a street fight.

Before drafting, read the current sender's voice engine in [character-voice-engines.md](references/character-voice-engines.md). Choose an emotional state and choose a pressure tactic from the available pressure modes that fit the current situation; do not turn either list into a template or mandatory phrase bank. Different cards from the same character must use different combinations of state and pressure instead of repeating the easiest signature.

Use canonical identities from current sources, for example: **Marketer @hype_queen**, **Sales @bigdeals**, **Designer @pixel_perfect**, and **AI Assistant @b2buddy_bot**. Never shorten a role into an invented label. If the PDF conflicts with Character Bible or current cards, use the current project sources.

The fact lock is Writer's boundary, not a style filter. Record: what happened; what did not happen; sender knowledge; people/objects that exist; allowed mechanics; and opinions/metaphors that are safe to invent. A metaphor, insult, aesthetic judgement, or self-serving spin may be new; an event, response, payment, opening, failure, public reaction, or object may not.

Write from what this person does to founder and team:

- **Marketer @hype_queen** turns an existing public moment into attention pressure; she may not invent a glitch, clip, pause, reaction, or campaign event.
- **Sales @bigdeals** flatters, guilts, reframes, or makes silence founder's fault; he may not invent an open, reply, payment, interest, or lead.
- **Designer @pixel_perfect** writes grammatical sentence case, treats taste as hierarchy, and makes a concrete aesthetic object into pressure; never borrow Dev formatting.
- **AI Assistant @b2buddy_bot** reports blind automation with sincere positive certainty; the harm is visible, but Bot is not knowingly writing a human punchline.
- **Dev @error404** may write lowercase, but stays understandable and names a human consequence instead of creating a technical riddle.

Do not optimize for checker findings, `or`, questions, line count, sentence count, button verbs, or fingerprint statistics while writing. Do not pre-emptively flatten a voice to avoid an advisory warning. Questions, `or`, emoji, profanity, and direct commands are available when this sender would use them.

Make the five variants genuinely different in action or pressure, not synonym swaps. Let a quiet variant remain characterful; “simple work message” never means anonymous office copy. Before the creative selection, ask:

1. Is the sender recognizable without the handle and job title?
2. Is there characteristic action or pressure, not only styling?
3. Does the message use the selected voice-engine state and pressure without copying another card's combination?

Writer selects the best 2–3 candidates on voice, situation, and force. Writer does not modify production files.

## Editor

Editor begins only after creative selection. It receives Writer's 2–3 candidates plus the fact lock and approved mechanics; it is a final gate, not a co-writer. Editor then reads the current sender's PDF section for a range check, never as a construction to imitate.

Editor does not rewrite candidates. Editor does not choose finalists. It may reject a candidate and state the reason; Writer must create a fresh replacement rather than edit toward a checker score.

Editor must not mask a weak situation with smooth English. If the action remains abstract after factual review, reject the situation and return it for simplification instead of polishing the copy.

Editor must not smooth slang, anger, mistakes, abruptness, profanity, or uneven rhythm merely to make English more polished. Reject only for a named fact, clarity, UI, repetition, or character contradiction. A cleaner version is not automatically a better version.

Check each selected candidate for:

- new facts, events, knowledge, entities, or claims beyond the fact lock;
- whether both buttons are understandable, mechanically allowed, prepared by the message, and about the same relevant object;
- four estimated visual lines, clear English, and no real brands or companies;
- direct copying from the PDF, public corpus, or current deck;
- whether the candidate sits inside the PDF's broad authorial range without borrowing its syntax, wording, event, or example shape;
- an explicit repeated line, opening, cadence, or button pattern already used by the deck;
- Character Bible/TOV contradiction, including Designer lowercase, a technical Dev riddle, a knowingly sarcastic Bot, invented Marketer publicity, or invented Sales evidence.

Run the checker only here:

```bash
node .agents/skills/mistakery-messenger-writer/scripts/check-messenger-copy.cjs --input /path/to/audition.json
```

Treat findings as questions, not automatic rewrites. The checker does not rewrite and does not choose finalists. It can flag formal risks but cannot prove fact truth or authorial force; perform the fact-lock and authorial checks manually. A zero share of questions or `or` is neutral, not an achievement.

Present surviving 2–3 candidates with the fact-lock support, button-consistency note, PDF-example calibration, and any remaining authorial risk. Do not write them to production.

## Feedback becomes memory deliberately

On an explicit approval or rejection, update [taste-memory.md](references/taste-memory.md). First classify the comment:

| Scope | Persist it when it means |
| --- | --- |
| Global | A rule of clarity, rhythm, ethics, or deck repetition |
| Character | A sender-specific motive, pressure, or voice constraint |
| Story | A fact or causal rule for one module/arc |
| One-off | A local choice for one card only |

Record the decision, why it worked or failed, scope, and technique used. An example is evidence of taste, not a template: never copy its syntax into a new card. Do not convert one-off feedback into a permanent ban.

## Final pass

- The situation is understandable without startup knowledge.
- The sender is identifiable without surface markers.
- The sender passes all three authorial-voice questions against the current sender's voice engine; Editor confirms only the broad PDF range.
- The card adds no brand, named entity, premature user/customer, or technical mystery.
- The two buttons execute different outcomes.
- Every literal event comes from the fact lock; new language is only opinion, metaphor, or insult.
- Message and buttons refer to the same relevant object and both choices answer the situation.
- The chosen cadence is not already doing the work of the joke elsewhere in the deck.
- The message contains no semicolon, and any profanity is earned by the emotional state rather than added for energy.
- Investor uses money and status pressure without coarse abuse.
- The five-draft set is not dominated by one line-count/final-type pairing, questions, `or`, the same ending command, or repeated button verbs; its plain work-chat option is characterful rather than deliberately bland.
- **Human check:** “Would a person send this in a work chat, or did a copywriter compose it for a card game?” Reject the latter even when it is clever.

If the checker is quiet but the message still feels like a copywriter explaining a game, reject it.
