### B3-VOICE-01 — Sales, four ignored emails

**Fact lock:** four emails were ignored; no open, reply, block, payment, interest, lead, or deal occurred. Sales knows the count and asks for three more. The only allowed actions are `Send three more` and `Leave them alone`. He may spin silence or guilt the founder, but not claim commercial evidence.

**PDF calibration:** short, mobile work-chat pressure; flattering address, a commercial spin, and responsibility pushed back to founder/team. No borrowed example syntax or deal event.

```text

That is not a rejection.
Let me send three more.
```

`S2`
```text

If we stop now, you look like you lost patience first.
```

`S3`
```text

Or I can turn four ignored emails into persistence.
Your call.
```

`S4`
```text

Give me three more, boss.
```

`S5`
```text
We have not heard back after four emails.
I am not writing off a prospect before it has disappointed me properly.
```

**Checks:** Every literal event is in the lock; both buttons answer the follow-up decision; all drafts fit four estimated visual lines. S2, S3, and S4 are recognizable as Sales without his handle because they turn silence into founder pressure, not a CRM update. None copies a PDF phrase.

**Finalists:** S2 (guilt), S3 (choice framed as Sales’s commercial spin), S4 (brash persistence).
