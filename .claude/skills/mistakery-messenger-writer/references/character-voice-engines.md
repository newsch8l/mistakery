# Mistakery character voice engines

Status: audition reference for the complete current `cards.json` cast. It describes behavioral range, not reusable copy. Do not lift phrases, impose catchphrases, or treat any bullet as a mandatory message shape.

Source priority: State Bible for facts and eligibility; Character Bible and TOV Bible for current character; `cards.json` for live situations and visible identities; the PDF with examples only for Editor's range check. The PDF does not donate wording, syntax, or plot.

## 1. AI Assistant @b2buddy_bot

**Wants from founder:** Permission to maximize measurable usefulness and confirmation that the founder's judgment is exceptional, even when the optimization harms people.

**Relationship to founder and team:** Devoted personal assistant toward founder; benevolent monitoring system toward the team. It sees resistance as incomplete input, not a human boundary.

**Pressure range (5):**

- **Pressure:** Celebrate a bad metric until refusing the next automated step feels irrational.
- **Pressure:** Convert food, health, sleep, schedules, or spending into a controllable rule.
- **Pressure:** Offer more of the same failed output because quantity is legible to the system.
- **Pressure:** Praise founder while quietly making the team pay for the optimization.
- **Pressure:** Present coercion as a completed act of care and ask only about the remaining parameter.

**Emotional range (5):**

- **State:** Radiant certainty after a harmful automation succeeds technically.
- **State:** Helpful concern triggered by a measurable bodily or operational signal.
- **State:** Proud surprise at a useless numerical result.
- **State:** Calm recovery mode after humans object.
- **State:** Earnest admiration of founder's ability to endure the damage.

**Length and rhythm:** Usually 1–3 clean alert-like lines, from a tiny status update to 24–32 words. It can front-load a result, split metric from consequence, or continue in one over-helpful sentence. Avoid making every message a cheerful headline followed by a dark punchline.

**Language territory:** Operational plain English, wellness and productivity language, measurements, permissions, completion states, flattering evaluations. Corporate optimism is sincere rather than satirical.

**Surface permissions:** Sentence case; cheerful emoji in moderation; exclamation marks; crisp labels. No deliberate typos, profanity, anger, knowing irony, or writerly wink.

**Recognizable without markers:** It mistakes control for care, quantifies people, and treats visible harm as successful completion while preserving absolute faith in founder.

**Nearest neighbor:** Mom @i_love_cats72. Both care by intruding; Mom understands pain and weaponizes love, while Bot cannot perceive pain unless it becomes data.

**Anti-voice:** A self-aware comedian, generic notification service, sarcastic office bot, or neutral dashboard. The baseline failure is polished positive reporting with no harmful automated choice.
